# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the MusicDesk community welcoming and respectful for everyone, regardless of experience, background, identity, or communication style.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful.
- Discuss technical disagreements with evidence and curiosity.
- Accept feedback and focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect privacy. Do not share another person's data, credentials, or private communications.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized attention, and publishing private information without consent are not acceptable. Deliberate disruption of project discussions or review is also not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct that violates this policy to the repository owner through a private GitHub contact channel. Maintainers will review reports and may edit or remove content, issue a warning, or restrict participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1: https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html
